# PartCraft Gifts — UGC Video Playbook

How to make UGC videos that sell an AED 59 tribute page to people in the UAE whose loved ones live far away.

Researched October 2026. Most of the benchmark figures below come from ad agencies and tool vendors, not independent studies. Use them as starting targets and replace them with your own numbers once you have 2–3 weeks of ad data.

---

## 1. The one insight that matters for this product

PartCraft does not sell an object. It sells **the moment the other person opens the link**. The site's own testimonials are already the best ads you have ("She cried for 20 minutes", "He opened it at 2am", "We hadn't spoken in 3 months").

So almost every video should do one of these three things:

1. **Show the moment.** The recipient opens the page and reacts.
2. **Show the sender's feeling.** The distance, missing someone, regret, a birthday they can't attend.
3. **Show how easy it is.** "3 minutes, AED 59, one link on WhatsApp."

Best results come from combining them: **sender's feeling (hook) → page being built or opened (demo) → reaction (proof) → price and CTA.**

---

## 2. What the research says works in 2026

### The first 3 seconds decide almost everything
- **Hook rate** = 3-second views ÷ impressions. One analysis of 479 Meta video ads found a median of about 21%, with the top 10% at 45% or more. Most guides call **30%+ good** and **40–50% top tier** for UGC.
- **Hold rate** = 15-second views ÷ 3-second views. Around 40–50% is average; **60%+ is strong**.
- Put the core idea on screen within 3 seconds and the main hook within 6 (this is TikTok's own guidance).
- Most Meta feed video is watched **muted**, so the hook has to work as on-screen text. Most TikTok viewers have **sound on**, so voice and music matter more there.

### Hook styles that are working now
| Hook style | Why it fits PartCraft |
|---|---|
| **Confession / sincere** ("I haven't seen my wife in 14 months.") | One vendor's H1 2026 data found confessional hooks converted well even when their hook rate wasn't the highest. It is the natural voice for this product. |
| **Relatable pain** ("Being in Dubai on your mum's birthday hits different.") | Rated the strongest UGC pattern. Keep it personal, a little vulnerable, never corporate. |
| **Skeptic → believer** ("I thought a website as a gift was cringe. Then she called me crying.") | Called the highest-converting format for many categories. It answers the "AED 59 for a link?" objection. |
| **Result first** (open on the crying or the phone call, then rewind) | Leads with proof. Works muted. |
| **Contrarian** ("Stop sending flowers abroad.") | Rated among the highest-retention openers, especially on TikTok. Use carefully, because it has to stay warm. |

### Structure
Most UGC frameworks use: **Hook (0–3s) → Problem / feeling (3–8s) → Solution / demo (8–20s) → Proof → CTA (last 3–5s).**
- Ideal length: **15–30s**. TikTok recommends 9–15s for non-Spark ads; Meta Reels work fine at 20–30s for emotional stories.
- Make the CTA specific: *"Make their page in 3 minutes, AED 59, link in bio"*, not *"check it out"*.

### Volume and variety beat polish (Meta Andromeda)
- Meta's ad system now picks who to show an ad to mainly from the **creative itself**, and it treats near-duplicates as a single ad. Ten colour versions of one video count as about one ad.
- Agency consensus: **6–8 genuinely different creatives per ad set minimum**, 15–25 when spend grows, refreshed every **2–3 weeks**. Expect roughly 1 in 10 to be a real winner.
- "Different" means a different **angle, person, occasion or format**, not a different font.
- Cheap way to iterate: keep one body and test **3+ different hooks** on it.

### Run ads from real people's handles
- **TikTok Spark Ads** (boosting a creator's or customer's own post) are reported by TikTok at +142% engagement, +43% conversion rate and −4% CPM versus normal ads.
- **Meta Partnership Ads**: Meta reports campaigns mixing these with normal ads saw 53% higher CTR and 19% lower CPA.
- These are platform-reported numbers, but the direction is consistent: ads that look like a person's post beat ads that look like a brand's ad.

## 3. Ten video formats for PartCraft

Ranked by how likely they are to work. Make 1–3 first.

1. **The reaction (with consent).** The recipient opens the link on a phone or video call; film their face, ideally split-screen with the page. Caption: *"Sent my wife in Manila a page for her birthday. Watch till the end."*
   - Risk: a flat reaction becomes the story (TikTok's "Couch Guy" effect). Only use clips where the emotion is obvious.
2. **Sender confession, talking to camera.** Filmed at night in a Dubai flat, car or balcony. *"I've missed 3 of my daughter's birthdays working here. This year I made her this."* → screen recording of the page → *"She called me in 10 minutes."*
3. **"I made this in 3 minutes" screen recording.** Fast POV: pick the occasion, add photos, pick music, record a voice note, send on WhatsApp. Hook: *"AED 59 and 3 minutes to make someone 4,000 km away cry."*
4. **Skeptic → believer.** *"My friend said a website is a lazy gift. Here's what happened when I sent one."*
5. **Apology story.** *"We hadn't spoken in 3 months. I couldn't say sorry on text, so I sent this."* The apology page is the most unusual feature you have, so give it its own ads.
6. **Cost comparison.** *"Flowers delivered to Kerala: AED 230 and dead in 4 days. This: AED 59, and she still opens it."* Keep prices accurate and current.
7. **Text-on-screen storytime** (no face needed). Slow pans of real photos with large captions telling the story, and trending emotional audio on TikTok. Easiest for shy customers.
8. **The voice note moment.** Focus on the "Add your voice" feature: *"She heard my voice the second she opened it."* Very strong for parents and grandparents.
9. **Green-screen reacting to messages.** A creator stands in front of a screenshot of the recipient's WhatsApp reply ("I'm crying at work 😭") and tells the story.
10. **Memorial (handle with care).** *"I made a page for my father so my kids would know him."* Soft, slow, no hard sell; the CTA is just *"Start their page."* Don't run these as aggressive retargeting.

## 4. Hooks to test (copy, adapt, translate)

Your audience badges list 🇦🇪 🇵🇭 🇮🇳 🇬🇧 🇺🇸 🇵🇰 🇱🇧 🇪🇬. Make versions in **Taglish, Hinglish/Hindi, Urdu, Malayalam and Arabic**, voiced by creators from that community. A native-speaker version is a separate creative in the platform's eyes, and it feels far more real to that audience.

**Distance / missing**
- "I live in Dubai. My wife is in the Philippines. This is what I sent her."
- "14 months since I last hugged my mum."
- "POV: you're 3,000 km away on their birthday."
- "Things OFWs don't post about: missing every birthday."

**Result first**
- "She called me crying 10 minutes after I sent this."
- "He opened it at 2am and couldn't sleep after."
- "My sister and I didn't speak for 3 months. Then I sent this link."

**Skeptic / curiosity**
- "I thought this was cringe until I saw her face."
- "Stop sending flowers abroad. Do this instead."
- "The AED 59 gift that beat my AED 800 one."
- "It's not a card, it's not a video. Watch what happens when they open it."

**Ease**
- "Made this on my lunch break."
- "No delivery, no customs, no waiting. Just a link."

Write each hook as on-screen text **and** say it out loud. Keep text in the top third of the frame.

## 5. Timing: the calendar is your targeting

Plan batches of creatives **2–3 weeks before** each moment:

| When | Angle |
|---|---|
| Valentine's Day (Feb 14) | Couples apart |
| Mother's Day in the UAE (March 21) | "Missing Mum" is your biggest emotional angle |
| Ramadan and Eid (dates move each year) | Family far away for Eid, plus apology/forgiveness themes during Ramadan |
| Father's Day (June) | Fathers working abroad, and children sending to fathers |
| Onam / Diwali / Christmas | Indian and Filipino communities; Christmas is huge for Filipino families |
| Month-end / payday | UAE salaries are usually paid at the end of the month, so push spend then |
| Always on | Birthdays, anniversaries, apologies |

## 6. Where to get the videos

1. **Your own customers (best and cheapest).** After someone pays, send a short follow-up: *"Did they love it? Send us their reaction and get your next page free."* Get **written consent from both the sender and anyone on camera** before using it in ads.
2. **Micro-creators from the communities you sell to:** Filipino, Indian, Pakistani and Arab creators in the UAE with 5k–50k followers, especially couples who are themselves long-distance.
3. **You, the founder.** A "why I built this" confession video is a legitimate format and costs nothing.
4. **AI avatar UGC:** possible for volume testing, but for an emotional, trust-based product it usually feels fake. Don't use it for reaction or testimonial formats.

### UAE rules (check before paying anyone)
- Since early 2026, anyone creating advertising content on social media from inside the UAE, paid or unpaid, needs a **UAE Media Council Advertiser Permit**. Brands are expected to check that the creators they work with hold one. Reported fines range from AED 5,000 to as high as AED 1M.
- Sources disagree on fees, dates and fines, so **confirm current requirements with the UAE Media Council** before you hire creators.
- Label paid content (platform "paid partnership" toggle plus #ad).
- Use **real** stories only. Don't script fake reactions or fake testimonials. That breaks platform rules and consumer law, and with a product built on emotion it destroys trust the moment someone notices.

## 7. The creator brief (one page max)

```
PRODUCT: PartCraft Gifts: a personalised tribute web page (photos, message,
music, voice note) for someone far away. AED 59 one-time, link sent by email,
shared on WhatsApp.

ANGLE FOR THIS VIDEO: [e.g. Mother's Day, sender confession]
WHO YOU ARE IN THE VIDEO: [e.g. working in Dubai, mum is in Kerala]

MUST SHOW
- Your face in the first 2 seconds, or the recipient's reaction
- Screen recording of building or opening the page (we can send a demo page)
- "AED 59" and "takes 3 minutes" said aloud or on screen

MUST NOT
- Fake reactions or invented stories
- Claim delivery of anything physical
- Mention competitors by name

DELIVERABLES
- 1 edited video, 20–30s, 9:16, 1080x1920, captions burned in
- 3 alternative hooks (first 3s), in your own words
- 1 version in [Tagalog / Hindi / Urdu / Arabic]
- All raw footage and B-roll in labelled folders
- No music baked in (we add licensed audio)

RIGHTS: paid ads on Meta + TikTok for [6/12] months, UAE + [countries];
we may re-cut and subtitle; whitelisting / Spark code required.
DISCLOSURE: #ad + paid-partnership toggle. Advertiser Permit no.: ______
DEADLINE: ____   REVISIONS: 1 round
```

## 8. Filming checklist

- 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, filmed on a phone (it should look like a phone video).
- **Safe zones (TikTok):** keep text and faces out of the top 160px, bottom 440px and 80px on each side. Keep the CTA clear of the right-hand button column.
- Natural window light or a lamp at night. Late-night lighting fits the "missing you" mood.
- Captions on every video, large and high-contrast, matching the gold-on-dark brand.
- Cut every 1.5–3s for the first 10 seconds. Don't leave any dead air.
- Show the **real product on screen**: the gold "Open Your Gift" screen is very distinctive, so make it a recurring visual signature.
- Use emotional piano or acoustic audio. On TikTok, trending emotional sounds help organic reach. For paid ads, use licensed or commercial-library audio.
- End card: *"Make their page in 3 minutes · AED 59 · Link in bio."*

---

## 9. Testing and measuring

**Weekly loop**
1. Launch 6–8 creatives: different angles × different people × 2 languages.
2. After roughly 1,000–2,000 impressions each, read the diagnostics:

| Metric | Diagnoses | Target | If low |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook rate (3s views / impressions) | The first 3 seconds | 30%+ | Change only the hook |
| Hold rate (15s / 3s views) | The story | 40–60% | Tighten the middle; show the reaction sooner |
| CTR (link) | The CTA and offer | ~1%+ | Clearer CTA; say the price |
| Cost per purchase | The whole ad | Under ~AED 30–35 to stay profitable at AED 59 | Kill it or re-cut it |

3. **Kill** losers fast. Make **3 new hooks** on every winner, then translate winners into the other languages.
4. Give each angle (distance, apology, memorial, ease, comparison) its own label so you learn which **angle** sells, not just which video.

Set the cost-per-purchase target from your real margin after Stripe fees and any refunds.

---

## 10. First 2 weeks: a concrete plan

1. **Day 1–2:** Founder films formats 2, 3 and 7 (confession, 3-minute build, text storytime). 3 hooks each → 9 creatives.
2. **Day 1:** Add a post-purchase "send us their reaction" request with a free-page incentive and a consent form.
3. **Day 3–7:** Book 3–4 micro-creators (one Filipino, one Indian, one Arab, one couple) with Advertiser Permits; run their posts as Spark / Partnership ads.
4. **Week 2:** Read hook and hold rates, kill the bottom half, make 3 new hooks per winner, and start the next seasonal batch (check §5).
